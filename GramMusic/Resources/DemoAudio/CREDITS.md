# Demo audio — provenance and licence

These clips exist so **demo mode** (App Review, `AppConfig.demoPhoneNumber`) plays real,
sustained music rather than a synthesized tone. Background audio is impossible to demonstrate —
to a reviewer or to anyone else — with an 8-second beep, which is what the mock backend used to
serve and why review could not locate the background-audio feature.

All six are excerpts (first ≤180s, 64 kbps mono, 5s fade-out) of recordings released into the
public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal**:

| File | Work | Source recording |
|---|---|---|
| demo1.mp3 | Bach, Goldberg Variations BWV 988 — Aria | Open Goldberg Variations |
| demo2.mp3 | Bach, Goldberg Variations BWV 988 — Variatio 1 | Open Goldberg Variations |
| demo3.mp3 | Bach, Goldberg Variations BWV 988 — Variatio 4 | Open Goldberg Variations |
| demo4.mp3 | Bach, The Art of the Fugue BWV 1080 — Contrapunctus 1 | The Art of the Fugue |
| demo5.mp3 | Bach, The Art of the Fugue BWV 1080 — Contrapunctus 2 | The Art of the Fugue |
| demo6.mp3 | Bach, The Art of the Fugue BWV 1080 — Contrapunctus 5 | The Art of the Fugue |

Performer: **Kimiko Ishizaka** (piano). The compositions are public domain by age; the
*recordings* were dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 by the Open Goldberg Variations
and Art of the Fugue projects, which is the part that matters — a public-domain composition does
not make an arbitrary recording of it free to ship.

- Open Goldberg Variations: https://archive.org/details/OpenGoldbergVariations (CC0 1.0)
- The Art of the Fugue: https://archive.org/details/pandacd-715-js-bach-the-art-of-the-fugue-kunst-der-fuge-bwv-1080 (CC0 1.0)

CC0 1.0 requires no attribution; this file is kept as a record of provenance, not as a licence
obligation. **Do not swap these for arbitrary "royalty free" audio** without equally documented
provenance — shipping a recording in the binary is distribution.
